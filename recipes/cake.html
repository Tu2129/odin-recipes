Cake

Description

A cake is a soft, fluffy dessert made with flour, sugar, eggs, and butter or oil. It can be enjoyed on its own or with frosting, fruit, or chocolate.

This recipe gives you a classic vanilla cake that is moist, light, and perfect for birthdays, celebrations, and other special occasions.

Ingredients

For Cake

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1½ cups sugar
  • 1 tablespoon baking powder
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • ½ cup unsalted butter (softened)
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract

For Chocolate Frosting

  • ½ cup butter (softened)
  • 2 cups powdered sugar
  • ½ cup cocoa powder
  • ¼ cup milk
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

Steps

  1. Preheat your oven to 180°C (350°F). Grease and line two 8-inch round cake pans with parchment paper.
  2. In a bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, salt, and sugar until well combined.
  3. In a separate large bowl, beat the butter and sugar until the mixture becomes light and fluffy. This usually takes about 3–5 minutes.
  4. Beat in the eggs one at a time, mixing well after each addition. Add the vanilla extract and mix.
  5. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the butter mixture, alternating with the milk. Mix gently until the batter is smooth. Avoid overmixing.
  6. Divide the batter evenly between the prepared pans. Bake for 25–30 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the centre comes out clean.
  7. Allow the cakes to cool in their pans for about 10 minutes. Transfer them to a wire rack and let them cool completely before decorating.
  8. Beat the softened butter until creamy. Add the powdered sugar and cocoa powder gradually. Pour in the milk and vanilla extract, then beat until smooth and spreadable.
  9. Spread the chocolate frosting between the cake layers and over the top and sides. You can decorate with chocolate chips, sprinkles, or fresh fruit.
